The story

In silence, true elegance speaks.في الصمت… تتجلّى الأناقة الحقيقية

Mash is an abaya label from Doha. Pieces are released in numbered drops and every abaya carries its own code, so there is never any confusion about which one you mean. When a piece sells out, it is gone; the next drop brings new ones.

The palette stays close to black, with the occasional grey, navy or print for the season. The character is in the finishing: embroidery, tassel closures, lace at the edge, the way a jacquard catches the light.
