Delivery, returns and terms

Ordering

Every abaya is identified by its code. You choose the size and length when you add it to your cart. Orders can be paid online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, or placed on WhatsApp.

After you order, Mash contacts you on WhatsApp to confirm the details and arrange delivery.

Delivery

We deliver anywhere in Qatar. Delivery costs 25 QR, and is free on orders of 2,000 QR or more. Pieces made to your chosen length usually take 5 to 10 working days; we will tell you the expected date when we confirm your order.

Returns and exchanges

Because each abaya is cut to the length you choose, we cannot accept returns for a change of mind. If your piece arrives damaged, faulty or different from what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp within 3 days of delivery with photos, and we will repair, replace or refund it.

Custom configurations requested in the order notes are final.

Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 14 days to show the refund.

Payments

Online payments are processed by Dibsy, a payment provider regulated by the Qatar Central Bank. Mash never sees or stores your card details.
